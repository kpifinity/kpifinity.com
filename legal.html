Privacy notice

How we handle your personal information.

LAST UPDATED · OCTOBER 2026

Who we are

KPIFINITY INC. (“KpiFinity”, “we”) is a Canadian corporation headquartered in Calgary, Alberta. We handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

This website does not collect personal information. It has no forms, analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. When you email us, we receive only what you choose to send — typically your name, email address, organization and message.

Why we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, to provide the services you ask for, and to keep reasonable business records. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Emails are kept in our business email account, with access limited to the people who need them to respond. Our team works from Canada and India; where information is accessed or processed outside Canada, it remains protected by this notice and by our contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and to maintain an ongoing relationship, then delete it or make it anonymous, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by writing to our Privacy Officer at hello@kpifinity.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.

Accessibility

Our accessibility commitment.

LAST UPDATED · OCTOBER 2026

Our standard

We design this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA — the same standard we apply to our clients’ products.

What we do

Readable contrast, keyboard navigation with visible focus, labelled form fields, descriptive link text, text alternatives for images, and layouts that adapt to screen size and zoom.
