The firm

A senior practice that delivers, and a studio that builds.

Kpifinity Inc. was founded in Calgary in 2018 by Rahul Kapai and Mitali Sharma. We have spent our careers inside complex, high-stakes systems, and we built Kpifinity to do that work on our own terms.

The story

From delivery, to data, to AI. Seven years in the making.

Kpifinity was founded on a simple premise: regulated industries needed senior practitioners who could manage complex programmes and design systems people actually used, not large firms staffing junior teams at senior rates.

Over time the practice deepened. Data science engagements added a second dimension. A pattern surfaced across sectors: organisations investing in AI without the governance, the delivery discipline, or the design rigour to make those investments work. That observation is now four practice areas and a published AI governance framework. Alongside the consulting, we began to build, first FinLII, and now SKI.

We are deliberately not in a hurry to separate the consulting from the building. The two feed each other, and that is the point.

Timeline

How we got here.

2018

Kpifinity founded

Programme delivery and design practice established in Calgary. First engagements in energy and financial services.

2019–2021

Depth across sectors

Embedded across government, energy, and financial services programmes. Data science begins: analytics platforms, reporting infrastructure, insight pipelines.

2022–2023

The AI governance gap

A repeated pattern: organisations investing in AI without the governance architecture to make those investments defensible.

2024–2025

The studio takes shape

FinLII explores cross-border investing. The SKI Framework, an open specification for defensible AI in regulated industries, is developed and published.

Now

Four practices, two ventures

An integrated consultancy and a working studio, with an associate network across each discipline.
