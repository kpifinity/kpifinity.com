SKI Framework

Sovereign AI for regulated industries: AI you can defend to an auditor, a regulator, or a court.

Regulated industries need AI in core operations but cannot use the tools everyone else uses. A cloud chatbot ships their data to a vendor and cannot prove how it decided. SKI closes that gap: an open, neuro-symbolic framework where a local model reasons over a signed knowledge graph of the actual regulation, an independent verifier catches what the model can hallucinate, and every decision can be reconstructed.

The thesis is simple. Defensibility in 2026 is not determinism or vibes. It is provenance. Where AI runs matters, and whether you can defend its decision matters more.

FinLII

Clarity for NRIs in Canada investing in India.

Cross-border investing is full of questions with no clear front door. Am I eligible? Which accounts do I need? What are the tax considerations? What is the right next step? FinLII helps Non-Resident Indians in Canada understand the full picture, eligibility, accounts, tax, and next steps, before they act. Clarity before action.

FinLII has slowed as SKI took the lead, but it is very much alive, and the cross-border financial workflows it explores keep teaching us about the regulated, money-meets-rules space we care about.

How the studio works

Built inside the firm, until it earns independence.

We do not separate our ventures into their own companies on day one. Keeping them inside Kpifinity lets us move fast, share what we learn between consulting and building, and formalise a venture only when it has earned that step. If SKI or FinLII grows enough to stand on its own, we will spin it out, and Kpifinity remains the studio that started it.
