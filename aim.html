AIM Partnerships · Advisor · Investor · Mentor

The partner your company needs — in exactly the roles it needs.

AIM is how KpiFinity backs startups and growing businesses. We act as your Advisor, your Investor, your Mentor — or any combination of the three. Sometimes we’re simply an advisor. Sometimes we’re all three. The roles follow your needs, and they change as you grow.

The three roles

Each role is a distinct commitment, with its own scope and its own terms.

AROLE 01

Advisor

Senior counsel on the decisions that shape the company — especially when you are selling into regulated, enterprise buyers.

  • Strategy, positioning and go-to-market
  • Product and UX design direction
  • Enterprise sales into energy, finance and public sector
  • AI, data and platform architecture
  • AI governance and compliance readiness
Typical form

Advisory retainer or advisory-board seat

IROLE 02

Investor

Capital from KpiFinity, committed where your thesis fits ours and we can add more than money.

  • Early-stage equity participation
  • Our own capital — aligned with your outcomes
  • Introductions to co-investors and enterprise buyers
  • Sweat-equity models where they fit
Typical form

Equity or advisory shares, sized case by case

MROLE 03

Mentor

Operator-to-founder guidance from people who have led large transformations and delivered products from zero.

  • Founder and leadership coaching
  • Delivery discipline, agile ways of working
  • Hiring and shaping the first team
  • Running the first enterprise pilot
Typical form

Regular 1:1 sessions with the founder

How a partnership forms

From first conversation to a written charter — then measured every quarter.

01

Conversation

You tell us where the company is and what’s in the way. We say candidly whether we can help.

02

Diagnostic

A short, structured look at product, market, team and runway to identify the gaps that matter most.

03

AIM charter

Roles, objectives, KPIs, time commitment and terms — agreed in writing before any work begins.

04

Review & reshape

Quarterly reviews against the agreed KPIs. Roles expand, narrow or end as the company changes.

Clear terms

Holding three roles demands more transparency, not less.

Every role in writing

Each role we hold has its own scope, term and compensation, set out in the AIM charter.

Conflicts disclosed

If we recommend KpiFinity’s own services or tools to a company we advise or invest in, we say so up front — and you are never obliged to buy.

Confidential by default

What you share stays with the people working with you. We don’t share it across our portfolio.

A clean way out

Every charter includes how a role ends, so either side can step back without damaging the company.

KpiFinity does not solicit investment from the public or offer securities. Any investment is made by KPIFINITY INC. or its principals directly, on terms agreed case by case and subject to applicable law.
