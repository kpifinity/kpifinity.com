Insights

A point of view on defensible AI, delivery, and design.

We write about the things we build and the rooms we have spent sixteen years in. A selection of our thinking follows. More is published in our newsletter, The Emerging Edge.

Thesis

Provenance, not determinism.

Defensibility in 2026 is not about a model being deterministic, and it is certainly not about vibes. It is about provenance: being able to reconstruct, after the fact, exactly how a decision was made, on what data, against which rule. Where the model runs matters. Whether you can defend its decision matters more. This is the idea SKI is built around, and the lens we bring to every AI governance conversation.

Regulation

The EU AI Act and DORA, in practice.

The headlines describe the rules. The harder question is what they require of a regulated operator on a Tuesday: which decisions need to be explainable, what evidence an auditor will actually ask for, and how to put AI into core operations without creating a liability you cannot defend. We translate the regulation into delivery, not slideware.

Build in public

Lessons from building SKI.

We build in the open. From the Regulatory Sentinel proof of concept to the framework specification, we share what works, what does not, and what we learned trying. It is the most honest form of credibility we know.
