About KpiFinity

Built on design. Proven in delivery.

KpiFinity was founded on a simple conviction: the best outcomes come when the people who design a product and the people who deliver it work as one team. Design and delivery are equal disciplines in our firm — and every engagement draws on both.

We design products and services, lead transformation, and put AI and data to work for organizations in energy, financial services, the public sector and health. Through AIM, we advise, invest in and mentor the founders building what comes next.
