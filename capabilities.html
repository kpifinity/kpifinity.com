Capabilities

Four disciplines, run by the people who scope the work.

Our practice grew across seven years inside complex regulated organisations. The disciplines operate together, because the harder problems require more than one to resolve.

01

Programme & Agile delivery

For: transformation leads, PMO directors, and sponsors of multi-team initiatives.

Senior programme leadership in environments where regulatory context, stakeholder complexity, and delivery failure each carry consequence. We turn ambiguity into a plan, and a plan into shipped work.

We run agile, SAFe, and hybrid frameworks without the bureaucracy, and we are equally comfortable recovering a programme that has lost its way.

  • Agile and SAFe programme leadership
  • Enterprise transformation delivery
  • Multi-stakeholder governance
  • Programme recovery and turnaround
  • Risk and compliance alignment
  • Backlog and product ownership
02

Data & analytics

For: data and analytics leaders, and executives weighing AI investment.

A decade of applied data science across regulated industries, from analytics platforms and reporting infrastructure to the governance foundations that determine whether AI investments are viable.

Our data practice is connected to delivery, not a separate division. The people who manage the programme also architect the platform it implements.

  • Data science and analytical modelling
  • Analytics and reporting platforms
  • Data architecture and governance
  • Insight pipeline design
  • AI-readiness assessment
03

UX & product design

For: product owners and operations leaders in compliance-critical settings.

Enterprise design for data-heavy applications and complex operational workflows, where an unclear interface is a compliance and operational risk, not only a frustration.

End to end, from the first interview to a shipped, accessible release, with design systems that scale across products and teams.

  • UX research, strategy, and design
  • Operational and compliance dashboards
  • Design systems and component libraries
  • Usability testing and iteration
  • Accessibility and inclusive design
  • Conversational and AI-native UX
04

AI strategy & governance

For: risk, compliance, and operations leaders adopting AI in core operations.

Grounded in years of delivery and data science, not a trend pivot. We help regulated organisations adopt AI in a way they can defend to an auditor, a regulator, or a court.

We are the architects of the open-source SKI Framework, and we bring that same thinking to client governance and adoption.

  • AI readiness and opportunity assessment
  • Governance and risk framework design
  • Vendor evaluation and due diligence
  • AI-augmented workflow design
  • Regulatory AI adoption strategy
